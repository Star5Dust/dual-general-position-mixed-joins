# Cover letter

2 September 2026

Dear Editor-in-Chief,

Please consider the manuscript "Dual General Position in the Join of a
Complete Graph and a Tree: Structure and Linear-Time Optimization" for
publication as a Contribution in *Discrete Applied Mathematics*.

For an integer \(r\ge 1\) and a tree \(T\), the manuscript determines the
dual general-position number of the mixed join \(K_r+T\) and gives a
constructive algorithm for a maximum set. The proof separates feasible sets
according to whether they meet the complete factor, reduces the tree-side
branch to exact local constraints, and turns those constraints into a
four-state rooted-tree dynamic program. The resulting computation and
maximum-set construction run in time linear in the order of the join.

The paper fits the journal's algorithmic discrete-mathematics scope by pairing
an exact structural classification with a proved linear-time optimization and
reconstruction algorithm. It is a graph-optimization result rather than a
claim of an external real-world application. The manuscript explicitly
identifies the previously published fan subfamily and distinguishes the result
from the closest available complete-first-factor preprint. Bounded exhaustive
and definition-first checks test the implementation and are reported as
supporting evidence, not as proof.

An earlier manuscript version and the reproducibility materials are publicly
available in the project repository cited in the manuscript. They have not
been peer reviewed or formally published. The manuscript is not under
consideration by another journal.

Thank you for considering this submission.
